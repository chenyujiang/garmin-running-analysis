## Outcome

Notebook: `analysis/notebooks/temperature.ipynb` (committed without outputs). As in 18, the analysis database is attached with DuckDB `READ_ONLY`, and its modification time is unchanged before and after; charts are replaced by bucketed tables and tables by month and by season. Data is first filtered by the rules confirmed in 18 (`pause_gap_s = 10`, GPS and altitude required, activities with more than 60 s accumulated at > 7 m/s per-second speed excluded): 1073 road runs, 614 valid, 612 after cleaning, of which 608 have air temperature.

**Watch temperature is unusable.** All 3363903 seconds of `records.temperature` are NULL. Checking the latest FIT with fitdecode: the FR245 Music's per-second records have no temperature field. So the weekly temperature line of the aerobic chart has always been empty, and neither "compare the two temperatures" nor "bias of watch temperature versus air temperature by season" can be done; below only Open-Meteo air temperature is used.

**Air temperature source.** Only the single 0.1° grid point with the most runs is requested (about 80% of runs with GPS fall in this cell, and the top few cells are adjacent to each other), covering the runs within ±0.2° around it (1062 road runs). Hourly air temperature is cached in the data directory under `raw/open-meteo/` (2021-08-06..2026-10-29, 45864 hours) and linearly interpolated at the midpoint of each run. The local Python's certificate chain fails to verify this site, so the system `curl` is used for downloading, with certificates verified as usual. The 5th/25th/50th/75th/95th percentiles of air temperature during runs: 11.3, 14.2, 16.8, 19.4, 22.5 °C.

**Aerobic fitness against air temperature** (608 runs, pace in s/km):

| Regression | Slope (s/km/°C) | R² |
|---|---|---|
| Pace against air temperature | 0.47 | 0.002 |
| Detrended residual against air temperature (minus a 365-day centred rolling median) | 0.34 | 0.001 |
| Mean heart rate against air temperature | 0.02 bpm/°C | 0.0001 |
| Within the same month (monthly means removed) | 2.98 ± 1.24 (95%) | 0.035 |

In 3 °C buckets, the median residual lies between −6 and +4 s/km, with no monotonic trend.

**Seasonal swing.** The monthly median of the residual ranges from −10.4 in February to +12.5 in November, a swing of about 23 s/km; but month explains only 5.4% of the residual variance. By season (southern hemisphere): summer −2.7, autumn −5.9, winter +0.5, spring +11.6. Spring (moderate temperatures) is slowest and summer and autumn fastest, the opposite of "slower when hot". The years also disagree: the spring residual for 2021..2025 is +17.7, +3.2, −1.0, +19.8, +10.7, and the summer residual −16.0, −2.3, +5.4, −17.6, +0.1. The seasonal swing looks more like the result of the training cycle (recovery in spring, race preparation in summer and autumn) than of temperature.

**Effect of correction** (reference temperature 17 °C, 202 weeks with at least 2 runs each):

| Method | k (s/km/°C) | SD of week-to-week change | SD of 4-week rolling change | Seasonal residual (summer, autumn, winter, spring) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No correction | 0 | 26.5 | 6.9 | −2.7, −5.9, +0.5, +11.6 |
| Full-sample slope | 0.47 | 26.5 | 6.9 | −4.8, −6.2, +1.8, +10.9 |
| Within-month slope | 2.98 | 26.9 | 7.6 | −15.2, −5.6, +9.4, +9.9 |

The full-sample slope is too small and the correction changes almost nothing; the within-month slope makes hot days within a month a bit slower, but the weekly series gets noisier and the summer–winter gap actually widens.

**Hot weeks.** 14 weeks have a median weekly air temperature ≥ 22 °C, and 40 weeks ≥ 20 °C. Weeks ≥ 22 °C have a median residual of +7.0 s/km versus +0.5 for the other weeks, a difference of about 6.5 s/km, but on only 14 samples.

**Recommendations** (awaiting user confirmation):

1. No regression correction: the full-sample R² is only 0.002, the seasonal swing is mostly not caused by temperature, and correction would make the weekly series noisier.
2. Use Open-Meteo as the air temperature source (the watch has no temperature). Change the aerobic chart's weekly temperature to the weekly median of air temperature during runs, fixing the temperature line that is currently always empty.
3. Mark hot weeks on the aerobic chart: median weekly air temperature ≥ 22 °C (about the 95th percentile of running temperature). The threshold goes into config.
4. If a reference temperature is ever needed, use 17 °C (the median air temperature during runs).
5. Open separate implementation tickets (`needs-triage`): add Open-Meteo air temperature fetching and caching to the analysis package (sending only the rounded grid point), switch the dashboard temperature to air temperature, mark hot weeks, and drop the dependency on `records.temperature`.

**The user confirmed all 5 recommendations on 2026-10-31.** Implementation: [22a Fetch Open-Meteo hourly air temperature on the import side](22a-weather-import.md) and [22b Switch the aerobic chart to Open-Meteo air temperature and mark hot weeks](22b-air-temperature-chart.md).
